GymManager User Guide

A walkthrough of every screen in GymManager — from creating your account to recording a member's payment. Use the menu on the left to jump to any section.

Creating an Account

Every gym is managed by an owner account. You'll need one before you can create a gym or add members.

To register

  1. Open the Register page.
  2. Fill in your full name, email, phone/WhatsApp number, and a password.
  3. Click Create Account.
  4. You'll be taken to the login page.
Use a real WhatsApp number — it's shown to members as your gym's contact number on receipts and payment messages.

To log in

  1. Open the Login page.
  2. Enter your email and password.
  3. Click Login.

Setting Up Your Gym

The first time you log in, you'll be asked to create your gym profile. This only happens once.

  1. Enter your Gym Name.
  2. Enter your gym's address (optional).
  3. Your account's phone number is shown automatically as the gym's contact number.
  4. Click Create Gym to continue to your dashboard.

Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is your home screen — a snapshot of your gym's current status.

What you'll see

  • Total members
  • Members paid this month
  • Members unpaid this month
  • Today's attendance count
  • This month's revenue

Quick Actions

  • Members
  • Add Member
  • Attendance QR
  • Membership Plans
  • Payments

Recent activity

The dashboard also lists members who haven't paid yet this month, and the most recent attendance check-ins, so you can act on both at a glance.

Adding a Member

Add a new member's basic details. You'll assign their membership plan separately.

  1. Go to Members → Add Member.
  2. Enter the member's full name (required).
  3. Enter their phone number — used for WhatsApp reminders and receipts.
  4. Enter their email (optional).
  5. Confirm or change the joining date.
  6. Click Add Member.
After adding a member, go to Assign Membership to put them on a plan — new members don't have one automatically.

Editing a Member

  1. Go to Members and find the member.
  2. Click Edit.
  3. Update their name, phone, email, or joining date.
  4. Click Save Changes.

Member Details

A full profile for one member — their membership, payment history, and attendance record all in one place.

What's shown

  • Personal information
  • Attendance this month
  • Current membership plan and expiry
  • This month's payment status
  • Full payment history, with receipt and WhatsApp buttons
  • Full attendance history

Open it by clicking Details next to any member on the Members page.

Activating / Deactivating a Member

Deactivating a member hides them from active lists and stops them from being charged, without deleting their history.

  1. Go to Members.
  2. Find the member and click Deactivate (or Activate, if they're currently inactive).
  3. Confirm the action when prompted.

Membership Plans

Plans define what you charge and for how long. Create at least one before assigning memberships to members.

Creating a plan

  1. Go to Membership Plans → Create New Plan.
  2. Enter a plan name (e.g. Basic, Premium, Gold).
  3. Enter the price.
  4. Enter the duration in months.
  5. Add a description (optional).
  6. Click Create Membership Plan.

Editing or deleting a plan

From the Membership Plans page, click Edit to change a plan's details.

A plan can only be deleted if no members are currently assigned to it. If members are using it, the delete option is disabled.

Assigning a Membership

Puts a member on a plan and sets their start date — the expiry date is calculated automatically.

  1. Go to Assign Membership (from the dashboard or Members page).
  2. Select the member.
  3. Select the membership plan — price and duration appear automatically.
  4. Confirm the start date.
  5. Click Assign Membership.

Attendance QR Mode

Turns a screen at your gym's entrance into a self-service check-in point. Members scan a QR code with their phone to mark themselves present.

Using it

  1. Open Attendance QR from the dashboard.
  2. Display the QR code on a screen or tablet at your entrance.
  3. Members scan it with their phone camera to check in.
  4. Today's check-ins update automatically and appear in the list on the right.
The QR code refreshes automatically every 60 seconds for security — no action needed on your part.

Recording a Payment

There are two ways to record a payment — from the general Payments page, or directly from a member's profile.

From the Payments page

  1. Go to Payments → Record Payment.
  2. Select the member/membership from the list.
  3. Select the month the payment is for.
  4. Choose Cash or Online as the payment method.
  5. Click Record Payment.

From a member's profile

Click Record Payment next to an unpaid member — their name, plan, and monthly fee are filled in automatically. Just confirm the amount and method.

A member can't be charged twice for the same month — if they've already paid, the option won't appear.

Payment Status

A month-at-a-glance view of who has paid, who hasn't, and whose membership is expiring soon.

Open Payment Status to see totals for paid, unpaid, expiring, and expired memberships, plus a full table you can scan member by member.

Receipts & WhatsApp

Every paid payment gets a printable receipt that can also be sent straight to the member on WhatsApp.

  1. Find the payment on a member's Details page or the Payments table.
  2. Click Receipt to view it, or Print Receipt to print/save as PDF.
  3. Click WhatsApp Receipt to open WhatsApp with the receipt details pre-filled — just hit send.
The WhatsApp button only appears if the member has a phone number saved on their profile.
